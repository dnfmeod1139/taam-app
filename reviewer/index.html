TAAM — Review Access Guide

TAAM is an invitation-only membership concierge for premium dining. This page shows exactly how to sign in with the demo account.
본 페이지는 앱 심사 담당자를 위한 로그인 안내입니다.

⚠️ Important — Do NOT enter an invite code

After tapping the red “Sign Up / Log In” button, the app shows an Invite Code screen. The demo account does not use an invite code.

→ Scroll to the bottom of that screen and tap “이미 회원이신가요? 로그인” (Already a member? Log in).

→ On the login screen keep the first tab “비밀번호” (Password) — selected by default — and sign in with email + password. Do not use “이메일 OTP” or “SMS”: they send a verification code you cannot receive.

About payments — why In-App Purchase is not used

  • All payments are for physical, real-world services consumed outside the app: an actual meal at a physical restaurant on a set date and time, plus our offline reservation-agency work.
  • The membership fee is for our offline concierge / reservation-agency service and a prepaid balance usable for those reservations. It does not unlock any digital content or app feature — all in-app content is available to every signed-in member.
  • Per App Store Review Guideline 3.1.5(a) (Goods and Services Outside of the App), these transactions must use payment methods other than In-App Purchase. We use Toss Payments (a licensed Korean PG) and bank transfer.
  • Age rating: the service is for adults 19+ (Korean legal drinking age) as reservations may include alcohol pairing.
