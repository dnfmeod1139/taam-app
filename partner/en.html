No. 1 — About

How TAAM works with restaurants

TAAM is an invitation-only membership app for premium dining across Korea, Japan and beyond. Members request tables through our reservation concierge, and TAAM stands between guest and house as the responsible party.

Every booking is secured by a member deposit held by TAAM — no walk-ins, no anonymous requests, ever.

No. 3 — Benefits

What your restaurant gains

01

Deposit-backed reservations

Bookings are made against funds already held by TAAM, with clear cancellation rules — the cost of no-shows stops reaching your door.

02

International guests, zero language friction

The app runs in English, Japanese and Korean; our concierge relays every request, so nothing is lost between guest and house.

04

No. 4 — Philosophy

A list that protects your standing

TAAM does not compete on volume. Only selected houses of genuine standing are listed — so the company your restaurant keeps on our list elevates its prestige, never dilutes it.

We are not built for one-off referrals. Our aim is the long relationship: good guests returning to good houses, season after season — and we intend to grow that circle together with you.
